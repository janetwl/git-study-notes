# 第三部分 · The Shift（时代转变：教育、知识、职业、互联网）

出处：Part III "CREATE YOUR IDEAL FUTURE" · THE SHIFT 节（含 EMERGING EDUCATION / SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE / CAREER CREATION / UNDERSTANDING THE INTERNET）。

## 定位：我们处在"第二个黄金时代"
- 教育/银行/政府正分别被独立教育者、加密货币、远程工作挑战；技术自由有代价：选择过载（"any given person is unaware of 95% of them"）。
- AI 与自动化威胁低技能劳动（快餐/卡车/写作/编程）："In times like these, it makes sense why everyone is losing their minds. Their survival is at stake in more ways than physical."
- 钱的底线论："Money is the lifeblood of society... you can play the status game of 'not needing to make a lot of money to be happy,' but unless you reject the world to live off in the woods, you are fooling yourself."
- 核心机会句：**"One man's labor cannot scale, but the creative ability of his mind can."** 教育、娱乐、启发构成数字社会的三支柱；"Purpose, profits, and peace are no longer mutually exclusive."

## EMERGING EDUCATION · 教育在贬值
- 学校的问题不是知识而是服从编程："the act of obedience without question is tattooed into our psyche"；学位="proof that you can do something that you hate for years"。
- 学校最糟条件反射：convergent thinking、conformity over authenticity、memorization over process、failure as something to avoid、authorities unquestionable、long work over efficiency、intelligence=book smarts。最好情况也只是一段帮你"进入黄金年时做更好决定"的经历。
- 理想教育次序（原文）："The first lesson of any teaching should be to encourage the questioning of any teaching. The second should be to lean into the curiosity that will allow you to fail the fastest. From there, we can include topics on persuasion, mental models, research skills, critical thinking, and entrepreneurship as vessels for applying any skill the individual may learn."
- 作者实证：大学编程课**两周自学 = 整个课程大纲**，翘课仍满分；2018 后行业只看技能不看学位。"You can learn any skill, cultivate your value, and distribute that value to the people that care about what you have to say."
- 上大学的真正原因诊断："The main reason people go to college is because they haven't questioned who they are, what they want, and developed themselves to the point of self-reliance."

## SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE · 专门知识（不可被训练掉的知识）
- 社会前 18 年系统性地剥走儿童好奇心——"A blessing for those that realize it, a curse for those that don't."
- **定义**："If you learn the same thing as everyone else to be trained into the select few jobs that are left, you can be replaced by almost anyone. The solution is specific knowledge." 它只存在于：vision + hierarchy of goals + 围绕目标的 curiosity-based self-education。
- "The difference between formal education and self-education is being able to choose the material you learn according to your self-generated goals."
- **zip file 隐喻**：专门知识=心智压缩包；独立教育者以特定视角传授，"specific information can be injected into their brain in a way that blooms their understanding"。
- 未来学校=创作者学校：学生随成长换"校"、最终开自己的校；"The teacher learns more than the student"；"People would stop creating to make money but would start making money to create."
- 教授 vs 创作者的差别=results："The creators solve their own problems, document the solutions, and package it up with a dash of personal experience." 判断在线课程真伪：basic critical-thinking skills；被骗过→学会选择谁可学。

## CAREER CREATION · 创造职业而非寻找职业
- **开篇定律**："Knowledge not found in schools is the source of money not found in employment."
- 数据锚（2023 视角）：85% of jobs that will exist in 2030 haven't been invented yet；60% of labor jobs will be automated within a decade。
- **未来工作要求**："The future demands that we earn with our mind, not our time. The only way to earn with your mind is to break out of the narrow-focused state of survival." 问题升级序列：local → social → global →（potentially）cosmic。
- Creator 定义："someone who becomes a fountainhead of value through personal development, shares that value in public, and makes a creative income doing so." 不限于线上。
- 三个时代：Industrial solved labor → Information solved knowledge → **Imagination Age will solve consciousness**。"You have the chance to be a part of this phase in human evolution."
- "People forget that they have the world's most powerful supercomputer between their ears. You must prioritize your mind. Mental mastery is how you fight back in a world that wants to steal your attention."
- 结果：个人发展的结果是"create your own career"。创作者经济样本（赤足鞋/木制厨具/蓝光眼镜/农场直供）：产品=作者自身问题的解，社区互相供养。
- "We are smack in the middle of both the Digital Dark Ages and the Digital Renaissance." 入场券：open mind + internet access + high-value skill stack。

## UNDERSTANDING THE INTERNET · 互联网的解剖
- 作者自白：一生主题是 obsession；silent observer（判断性→明辨性观察："How did that person get to that point in life?"）。
- 常规职业路径讽刺（school→18 岁选终身职业→70% 的一天不受控→65 岁才自由）："Sign me up! Nope."
- 作者试错史：健身房+社媒学习+技能学习，**fail 7+ business models**——"To many, this sounds like a hellish existence. To me, it was the only thing I could find true satisfaction in."
- 未来工作句："The future of work will not be work. It will be play." ** entrepreneurship 的重新定义**："It's not about starting the next billion-dollar company. It's about leveraging your stack of skills, unique story, and finite focus to create a life of meaning. Everyone is an entrepreneur. Some just get paid for the value they cultivate outside of the conditioned path."
- 两种多巴胺："Meaningless dopamine sources have people in chains, while meaningful dopamine is reserved to those that hunt and evolve."
- **互联网解剖**：back end = code（不深究）；**front end = media（每个人都该掌握，因为"it is a microcosm of life itself"）**。Media = writing + speaking + visuals 分发 valuable message；"Media is an extension of the personal and collective mind." 影响性媒体模仿宇宙的涨落（capture attention, hold attention, deliver value on attention —— 通过 problems, stories/games, solutions）。
- 互联网四身份：public résumé / school / job board / podium（"if you perceive it as such"）。
- 四类玩家：brands & creators（教育-娱乐-启发受众；没有产品则自我宣传不浪费；雇主在线上招"分发价值最多的人"）→ students → consumers（定义：**无 vision、消费表层信息、不利于其不存在之理想未来的人**）。"Everyone is a consumer, yes, but... one must also become a creator so that extreme consumerism doesn't overtake their potential."
- 收尾："You either create and distribute value or drastically limit the opportunities available to you." 理解人性的最大导师=observation, awareness, correcting your focus through self-reflection。

## 辅导提示
- 用户担心职业被 AI 淘汰 → earn with mind not time；specific knowledge；local→cosmic 问题升级序列。
- 用户纠结要不要回学校/考证 → 学位=服从证明论 + 两周自学案例 + 创作者教育替代路径。
- 用户想做内容但不知从哪 → 互联网四身份 + front end=media 是必修课 + creator 定义（先从个人发展出发）。
