# 方法卡 · 反愿景→愿景协议（Anti-Vision to Vision）

来源：Part II · THE FORMULA · ANTI-VISION（见 ch07）。这是 The Formula 的第一步。

## 何时使用
- 用户说"不知道自己想要什么/人生没方向"。
- 用户失去动力，需要一个"不可能被分心的 frame"。

## 原理
**"It's easier to know what you don't want - from experience - than it is to know what you want - from imagination."** 想象需要经验做燃料；两者合并后"you create a frame that is impossible to distract your focus."

## 流程（需纸笔/笔记本，书中反复要求"pull out a journal"）
1. **残酷觉察两件事**：(1) 你不想要什么；(2) 照现在这样继续下去你会落在哪里（去观察人群："Observe the masses and see where mindless action leads. It's not pretty."）。
2. **写下反愿景**："Write out every single thing you don't want, why you don't want it, and how you are going to avoid it."
   起始提问（答案必须自己生成，卡片不许代答）：
   - 对你而言"不充实的一天"长什么样？
   - 你会避免成为什么样的伴侣？
   - 你不想长成、感觉成、行为成什么样？
   - 多少钱少到无法支撑你的生活？为什么不够？那时候你做不了什么？
   - 你讨厌生活在什么环境里？为什么？
3. **越具体越好，到不舒服为止**："Get meticulous here... when you feel thoroughly uncomfortable, transmute that negative energy toward the opposite."
4. **翻到新页写愿景**：回答上面同一组问题的正版本。然后"dance between the 2 to make better decisions daily."
5. **愿景规格**：big、不被现有 belief 限制、"emotionally compelling when you marinate your mind in the growing detail"；它应写在你的步态里（"Your vision is your gift"——不是钱/车/假期本身）。
6. **维护节奏**："Come back to your writing at least once a month to revise and remove limits on your potential that you had not seen before."

## 情绪引擎（补充燃料，非必需）
- 作者样板：想象父亲临终/回忆最恨之事——"Anyone can dig into the experiences of their past, generate a potent energy source within them, and focus that energy though action toward an intrinsic hierarchy of goals."（though 系原书拼写，应为 through）
- 自纪律定义："Self-discipline is when the desire to become the highest version of yourself outweighs the desire for cheap pleasures."

## 后续
反愿景+愿景定完后接 ppp 卡（Purpose-Path-Priority 拆解），不要停在想象层面。

## 边界
- 答案慢是正常的："It may take weeks, months, or years to get answers to your burning internal questions. This is natural."
- 负向能量只作燃料不作身份；愿景每月修订——它是活的，不是契约。
