# 方法卡 · 建造者学习循环（Builder Loop：Start, then learn）

来源：Part III · THE PROJECT（见 ch11）。含 HOW TO LEARN / BECOME A BUILDER。

## 何时使用
- 用户"学了很多不会用"、教程地狱、囤课囤干货。
- 用户想启动一项新技能/新副业但没有项目载体。
- 用户只有被分配的任务，感受不到 fulfillment。

## 反模式（书中判词）
- 囤理论："We love to horde theory, orthodoxy, and conceptual understanding of things without direct experience."
- 教程地狱：作者学设计时看尽软件教程→实战"我什么都不知"→"I had wasted weeks of time 'learning' just to end up at ground zero."
- 高级阶段还靠外部指导是错觉："true experts rarely need guidance... intuition and direct experience trump all once you get into the advanced stage." 卡住者被攒下的概念反过来操纵（"gain a shred of sentience and become their puppet master"）。

## 正模式：项目=容器
**"You need a real-world project to act as a vessel for your exploration of the unknown. A project helps narrow your focus on one achievable goal, brings clarity to your actions, and encourages true learning through struggle."**

### 科学项目六步（书中原序）
> "form a hypothesis, identify a problem, research how to solve it, document your discoveries, refine your system, and publish the solution."

### Builder 换序法（学任何新技能的操作）
1. 先 outline 一个具体项目（要做出来给谁用/解决什么问题）。
2. 找一个你 admire 的同类项目作参照（"Success is a game of intelligent imitation"）。
3. 按当前卡点从 beginner→advanced 顺序**按需**检索专门知识，"in a way that allowed me to apply the information immediately."
4. 允许从第一天就 struggle（"I prime my brain to receive an immediate lesson through my specific research"）。
5. 找到解法=quick win → 动量滚入项目其余部分。
6. **Start, then learn.** "If you want to truly learn, theory must brush up against reality."

### 学习机制配套（让循环加速）
- **Intention 前置**："Intention is the invisible bridge between goal and action." 项目目标顶在心头，signal-to-noise 自动改善（dopamine  novelty + pattern recognition：买车效应/新词效应）。
- **Law of Use**："If you don't use it, you lose it."——项目就是"用"。
- **Law of Inspired Action**：灵感"has a short fuse"→必须配 idea-capture 系统（笔记本/App；"you won't remember it without writing it down"）。
- **Protegé Effect**：边学边教——"When you teach what you know, and struggle to articulate certain concepts, you reveal gaps in your knowledge... regardless of how much of an imposter you think you are."
- 人类最优学习全链（背下来照着排）："Humans learn best when they pursue a goal, encounter a problem, remain conscious of that problem, notice information that can solve that problem, rapidly act on that information, and go on to teach what they learn to others."

## 项目复利层
- 解出的方案双用途：释放你的心理能量→做更深的东西；传给需要的人→提升集体意识/赚收入/两者。
- **"You are the infinite project. You are your life's work."** 持续流的新项目=生命的新鲜感来源（作者模式："a perpetual stream of evolving personal projects to work on first thing brought novelty to my days"）。
- "Projects only live on as long as their impact is spread."——发布是循环的最后一环，不可省。

## 边界
- 别认同技能，要认同你在解决的问题（ch11 作者反思）。
- 被分配的项目（学业/客户/工作）不等于废——"necessary, but they didn't bring me the fulfillment"；在它们之外保留"属于自己的第一个时段"。
