# 方法卡 · 已知边缘推进法（Edge of the Known / 蜡烛模型）

来源：Part II · THE FORMULA · THE EDGE OF THE KNOWN（见 ch07）+ ch01 TACTICAL STRESS + ch06 PATH OF MASTERY。

## 何时使用
- 用户停滞：要么无聊（在旧舒适区待太久），要么焦虑（目标投影过远）。
- 用户问"进步好慢/什么时候是个头"。
- 用户被生活卡死需要电击（谨慎，此为急用版）。

## 蜡烛模型（书中心智图）
未知=大黑房间；愿景=逐渐变亮的微光；**你的目的=远处烛光；目标层级=从目的一路排到你面前的一排蜡烛——越近的蜡烛越亮，亮=你有技能接下的挑战层级。**
- "If the candle is bright and close, you will not get anxious, but if you stay there for too long, you will get bored. **Fulfillment is found at the edge of the known.**"
- 诊断：焦虑=在朝远处的暗烛投影（无教育/技能/挑战去搭桥）→ 回到最近的蜡烛；无聊=在已点亮的近烛待太久 → 向下一个黑暗格挪一格。

## 推进操作
1. **最小组合片原则**（ch07 举重例）："The smallest plates are the most conducive to progress... add the smallest amount of difficulty to sustain growth over time."——每次只加最小增量。
2. **Tiny compounding improvements**：向愿景做微小复利改进；别人说 "You've changed" 时的标准答复："Yes, the tiny improvements are starting to add up."
3. **心理时间排毒**（ch07）：察觉自己"早晨醒来就预演压力一天"= "we psych ourselves into having yet another bad day." 破法=当天设一个新意识目标，想 new thoughts、set new goals、learn new skills、execute。
4. **平台期导航**（ch06）："Where most people quit is where greatness is born." 感知阈值处=心智被迫扩张处；"You cannot solve a problem from the same level of mind that created it."
5. **时间尺度检验**（ch06）："If you can't see yourself doing something every day, why are you doing it? If you say you want to do this work for 5 to 10 years, why does it matter if you don't get paid for the first 6 months?"

## 急用版：Tactical Stress（战术压力，慎用）
定义："The conscious decision to put yourself in a do-or-die situation, knowing that you have the skill to make it work, and will accept nothing less than success."（ch01/ch07）
- 动作菜单（书中原列）："Pull an all-nighter. Sign up for a marathon. Book a flight across the globe. Move to a location that demands 3 times more than what you can afford... Ask the client for double your normal rates."
- 前提与警告：仅当技能已匹配、只是 fear 挡路时有效（"the only thing stopping you from reaching the next level is fear"——ch01）；**"I do not recommend doing it often."** 常规路线仍是慢而一致的进步（扩大指数增长的表面积）。

## 边界
- 探索未知要"with curiosity as your driver toward a meaningful goal"（ch01），不是盲目冒险癖。
- 风险悖论提醒（ch07）："Big aspirations but low risk tolerance... the default path presents the greatest risk of them all: staying the same just like everyone else."
