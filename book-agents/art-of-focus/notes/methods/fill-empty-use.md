# 方法卡 · 填-空-用每日节律（Fill / Empty / Use）

来源：Part III · THE NEW RICH · FILL EMPTY USE + THE 4-HOUR WORKDAY（见 ch09）。

## 何时使用
- 用户创造力枯竭："输入很多写不出东西"、"卡在问题上"。
- 用户日程全是工作没有休息，或反过来用"自我关怀"麻痹。
- 用户想重建一天的结构。

## 三段定义（书中原文顺序）
> "Fill your mind in the afternoons with books, learning, and conversation. Empty your mind with walks, training, and contemplation. Use your mind in the morning with creation, output, and focus."
（时段是作者个人偏好——"If your energy is highest in the morning, schedule your work around that." 按自己能量峰值重排，三段结构不变。）

### FILL（填=有意识输入）
- "You cannot create if you don't consume." 带 vision/goal/problem 滤镜输入，不是为学材料而学。
- 起步配方：重读那本改变你的书→挑一个没懂的题→按推荐找资料→循环到攒出 5-10 个源，形成你的"人生哲学"底料。
- 留存法：和任何人进行智力对话（说出发现、暴露知识缺口）。
- 建 idea-capture 系统（ch11 法则："inspiration has a short fuse"——不写下来就丢）。

### EMPTY（空=让 DMN 工作）
- 依据（书中转述 Alex Pang《Rest》）：Default Mode Network 在停止外部专注时激活，"the brain isn't any less active than it would be when engaged in something like focused work."
- 实操清单（ch09）：长走 / 泡在自然 / 健身训练 / 睡前把想法-计划-强迫念头 brain-dump 到白纸。全天时段做实验找最配你的组合。
- **真休息 ≠ 现代 self-care**："true rest does not include emotional binge eating, bubble baths with wine, and late-night movies to escape the thoughts of work."
- 卡壳时最高级用法=放手让生活给答案："Rather than writing to get the best reaction out of your reader, just write... when you are stuck on a problem, the best thing you can do is let reality win."（程序员放下 bug 后 shower thoughts 突袭=同款机制）
- 作者版：drop everything but the pillars of my life → see what sticks without conscious effort → "Like magic, the idea I needed comes to me within a few days, and births a season of intensity."

### USE（用=窄焦输出）
- 高能量时段做 creation/output/focus；配套 4 小时工作制与专注肌肉训练（见 value-ladder 或本卡附加）：
  - **"Four hours of focused work is better than 8 hours of distracted work."**
  - 从 15 分钟计时纯专注起步→30→45→60 分钟（"Like building a muscle"）；3-5 小时/日是心理能量甜点区。
  - 只搬 lever-moving tasks："If you aren't getting results, that is feedback to change what you are doing."
  - 内外双侧防干扰：早起一小时抢在干扰前；工作时把理想生活方式整体保持在心里。

## 节律观
"Productivity and creativity are not one-time things, they are states of mind on the spectrum of focus." 目标是 deep play + deep rest + deep work 的平衡与程度，不是某个 morning hack。**"Living fully is a lost art in a world that overvalues the surface."**

## 边界
- "This isn't a one-and-done type deal. This is a lifelong series of experiments that may never reach perfection but will always reach a higher level of enjoyment."
- 强度不可持续（ch09）："Intensity is powerful, but not sustainable." 排程先保 consistency 再谈 productivity。
