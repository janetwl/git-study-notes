# 方法卡 · 心智家务（Mental Housekeeping：生活重启 SOP）

来源：Part III · THE NEW RICH · LIFESTYLE DESIGN（见 ch09）。

## 何时使用
书中自述触发条件："This is a process I run through when life gets a bit too messy. Like when you're constantly thinking back to a time when 'life was better.' Or when your routines succumb to entropy, fall apart, and all these little tasks pop up that suck up the little time you have in a day."
即：routine 崩坏、杂务淹没、怀念过去、忙而无果。

## 四步流程
1. **审计（10 分钟，掏日志）**："take note of how you feel. Write down exactly what you are doing on a daily basis. Every little detail. Take 10 minutes and get specific on what you do, why you do it, and how you feel in the morning, afternoon, and night."
   目的："identifying unconscious parasites (time and energy suckers)."
2. **三刀（Prioritize / Remove / Restructure）**：
   书中给出的病因三分法——"The reason you aren't making progress is because you feel pressed for time, don't have the energy to get results, or you stopped doing the things that got results."
   - Prioritize the things that will get results.
   - Remove the things that snuck their way into your day.
   - Restructure specific tasks and obligations to free up more time.
   （"Get the chaotic structure of your mind on paper and reorganize it."）
3. **一周系统**："create a weeklong system... Write out every single thing you are going to do for the next week. Keep this simple and don't overthink. Just jot it down. **This is how you reduce the friction of making better decisions.**"
4. **四套 routine + 迭代**：规划 morning routine / focused work routine / 杂务与会议 / nightly routine。"If the routine doesn't flow, go back to your notebook and refine the system."

## 原理句
- "You are in this chaotic state because of psychic entropy, disorder of the mind. The organization of your life tends toward disorder if you don't perform mental housekeeping."
- 终局状态："With this mental housekeeping process, you gain as much control as you can over your day."
- 对暴露出的"阻碍 Enjoyment 的部分"："Treat these as problems in your system that will be solved through focused education, experimentation, and effort."（= 一次搞不定就转 ppp/bulk-cut 卡继续解）

## 边界
- 本 SOP 是"重启"不是"优化癖"——目标是把日程填向 enjoyment 与 results，不是塞满。
- 配套心态：习惯难断，靠清晰化减摩擦，不靠意志力神话。
