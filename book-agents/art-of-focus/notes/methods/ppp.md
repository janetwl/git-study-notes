# 方法卡 · PPP：目的-路径-优先级（Purpose Path Priority）

来源：Part II · THE FORMULA（见 ch07）。The Formula 的执行引擎。

## 何时使用
- 用户有模糊方向但没有落地动作；或计划过多不知先做哪个。
- 用户焦虑/淹没（诊断句："You are projecting too far into the future. You don't have the education, skill, or conscious challenge to bridge the gap."）
- 用户决策瘫痪。

## 三步拆解
### 1. PURPOSE（目的）
- 定义：**"A purpose is a meaningful goal. From that goal stems a problem that you must focus your attention on solving."**
- 定位当下目的：**"Your purpose is the most pressing problem in your life at this very moment. The one that you have been putting off addressing."**
- 不可跳阶："Purpose evolves from superficial to metaphysical and you cannot skip steps." 想做大事先问：生存问题（尤其钱）解决了吗？没解决就先解决它（也见 value-ladder 卡 cashflow 优先）。
- 目的会演进："This is your purpose until the next reveals itself." 别把身份焊死在一个目的上。

### 2. PATH（路径）
- 把大目的挂到愿景上，再**笔记本拆成年→月→周→日目标**："break this big goal, or purpose, into a clear path... Spend time writing out yearly, monthly, weekly, and daily goals that will actualize the accompanying purpose. Don't get caught in the technicalities of goal setting. Be specific, be actionable, be vain if you want to."
- **四领域各设一个大目标**：health, wealth, relationships, happiness（"the 4 domains that allow you to master your survival"）。不必立刻全部行动——它们供视角与愿景笔触。
- 目的=滤镜：把 purpose 保持在心里，它会自动重组你对信息的感知（甜点说不例、读书滤镜例）。
- 目的演进后重复整个"清晰度生产"流程。

### 3. PRIORITY（优先级）
- **Lead domino**："Your priority actions to actualize your goals are ones that get results... The levers that pull you toward your desired outcome regardless of the amount of time they take to perform." 每问自己：哪一块倒下会带倒其余？
- 现实约束优先："If you have financial problems but can't start a business due to having too many responsibilities, then don't start a business. Face reality"——先做当下技能/处境够得着的小目标（预算法/技能升级/换岗），动量起来再上台阶。
- 淹没时降级："Creating a lesser goal that matches your current skill level can absolve all doubt in your ability to act."

## 执行诊断口诀（迷失三连）
> "If you're lost, the answer is education. If you're educated, the answer is execution. If you're executing, the answer is consistency."

- 没知识→教育；有知识不动→执行；执行不稳→一致性。永远存在"下一步"。

## 决策兜底
- 用计划当滤镜做决定；"if you can't make a choice, the answer is no, which often means doing nothing to let the fabricated problem blow over."
- 系统视角：系统=desired outcome + process + inputs；每次解决一个问题就把它沉淀成系统（迭代到"自动化"），然后腾出能量给下一个更难的问题。

## 边界
- 激情后置："Progress is a better metric to focus on"——别等 passion 才执行。
- 时间成本："If this sounds too time consuming... I would ask you to question the distraction attempting to penetrate your mind at this moment. Nothing is more important than your potential."
