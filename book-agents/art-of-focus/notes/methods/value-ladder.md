# 方法卡 · 独立收入阶梯（Value Ladder：技能栈 → 一人企业 → 产品）

来源：Part III · THE SHIFT / THE SKILL STACK / THE PROJECT / THE VESSEL（ch08-ch12 汇总）。

## 何时使用
- 用户想赚独立收入/做副业/一人企业但不知从哪开始。
- 用户"没成绩不敢教"、纠结利基、做了产品没人买。
- 用户问"学什么才不会失业"。

## 前置诊断（顺序硬约束）
- **"Cashflow is often the first problem that should be prioritized, because a lack of it places a limit on how far any other area of life can be developed."**（ch12）
- 不可跳阶：生存没稳住先解决生存（ch07）；"It is close to impossible to act with authenticity when your actions are influenced by need of something outside of yourself."

## 第一层：学什么
- 时代判断：85% of 2030 的工作尚未发明 / 60% labor jobs 十年内被自动化（ch08）→ **"The future demands that we earn with our mind, not our time."**
- 防替代="specific knowledge"（ch08）：跟别人学一样的东西=可被任何人（和机器人）替代；专门知识来自围绕自设目标的 curiosity-based self-education。
- 三分法（ch10）：
  1. **Evergreen 消息技能**：marketing（为特定 identity 造 attention-grabbing+relevant+valuable 的消息）+ sales（让人意识到问题→看见影响→给出达成其目标的方案）。
  2. **Evergreen 媒介技能**：writing（先写）+ speaking（写增强说）。"In life, discipline equals freedom. In business, distribution equals freedom."
  3. **Results-oriented 技能**（随时代换）：email/设计/视频/编辑/动画/自动化/社媒。
- 组合公式（ch10 立方模型）：4 永恒市场（health/wealth/relationships/happiness）× evergreen 技能 × results 技能 × 个人兴趣 → 把问题放在立方中心，边缘取火力拼创造性解法。
- 学习方式：builder 卡（Start, then learn）。

## 第二层：卖什么形态（DFY→DWY→DIY，ch12）
1. done for you（自由职业/代做）——最快但一人封顶；
2. done with you（教练/咨询/tutor）——"earn with the creative ability of our mind"，帽略高；
3. do it yourself（课程/软件/模板/书/订阅）——"earn an income while you sleep. That is the new luxury for the individual of the 3rd millennium."
- 用法："experiment with each one according to your experience level, pivot into the next."
- 价值三放大器：出结果的 system + 个人 reputation + 过往客户结果。

## 第三层：起步路径二选一（ch12）
- **路径 A（最快现金）**："learn a skill, teach the skill, sell the skill."（作者近万品牌咨询经验背书："the fastest path to cash, no gimmicks."）
- **路径 B（一人创意企业）**："pursue your goals in public (brand), solve your problems as you are pursuing those goals (content), and create a system to help others do the same (product)."
  - **从低价服务起步不从产品起步**（产品冷启动没有分发只能烧广告）："With a problem-oriented service and sales know-how, you can generate a full-time income with 2-4 clients a month, rather than one hundred to five hundred product sales."
  - 出结果→把服务沉淀为产品→卖给养了数月-数年的 audience："it will sell."
- 现实预期：顺利的话 6-12 个月出现"belief-shattering results"（"Considering everything goes right, which it won't"）。

## 第四层：定位（ch12）
- **"You have $100,000 trapped in your head."** "The most profitable niche is you... The authentic don't have to compete with anyone but themselves."
- niche 钉在**别人想要的 goal** 上（不是 problem 上），为其造 system，随其技能升级引入新挑战（=gamify 你的产品/服务）。
- 判分标准："**Results are determined by behavior change.** If your customers don't use your information to better move within the world, you did not do your job."
- 教学位阶：山地图像——你只需帮"落后你几步的人"，这正是最优点位（不需要等到登顶；职业选手没 system 也教不了新手）。
- 不需要结果才能开始："People find value in those documenting their journey to achieve a goal that they have."

## 心态护栏（书中原句）
- "If you don't sell your own product, you will be forced to sell someone else's."
- 反饱和焦虑：本方法"bypasses all of them by leveraging the most unique thing on this planet: you."
- "success is counterintuitive, and the quickest fix is the longest path."
- 总纲："The path to the good life is simple: actively pursue your ideal lifestyle, create solutions to problems along the way, and get paid for sharing that solution with others."（ch11）
- 长期性："One percent of fifty years is 6 months, and most people can't commit to the uncertain path for more than 2 weeks."（ch10）+ "Nothing happens, then everything happens."；"Too many people quit before this phenomenon occurs. They believe that understanding is linear when it's exponential."
