# 方法卡 · 拉远-投入循环（Zoom-Out SOP）

来源：Part I · THE UNIVERSE（AT ONE NOT WAR，见 ch03）+ Part II · THE LENS（MAKING BETTER DECISIONS，见 ch06）。

## 何时使用
- 用户陷入精神战斗：反刍、愤怒、恐惧、与情绪较劲。
- 用户把问题看得天大、在细节里出不来。

## 标准流程（书中原文顺序）
1. **不战斗**："When mental battles are at your doorstep, don't fight with it, flow with it."（ch06）
2. **暂停-拉远-观察**：Pause, zoom out, observe. 问三个放大问题（ch06）：
   - 十年尺度/宇宙视角下这事还剩多少？"If you don't feel like getting out of bed, zoom out and realize how measly your problem is from a higher perspective."
   - 这事在系统的哪个环节、如何影响更大整体（蜂蜜追踪法）？
   - 对方的行为放回他的目标里看得解释吗？（"Everyone may seem wrong, but when you take their conscious or unconscious goals into account, they are right."）
3. **三步和解（ch03）**："Awareness is the first step, universal perspective is the second, and if it is real, accepting the battle for what it is comes last."
4. **重新投入执行**："when you gain clarity through the intangible resources you've acquired, dive back in and execute with focused effort."

## 配套区分
- **桑拿悖论**（ch03）：同样的刺激（热），天气里骂娘、桑拿里享受——差异是 expectation 与 intention。所以痛苦 = 现实与"应该"的缝隙（EXPECTATION VS REALITY, ch01）。
- **感受是反馈不是指令**："You can write, and you can write well, regardless of how you feel."与"Feelings are feedback, and we should treat them as such."（ch03，两句之间原文有举例句）拉远后行动照旧。
- 顺境也要用："Be wary when things are going too well."（ch03）舒适期=为下轮高温储备的休息。

## 边界
- 拉远不等于压抑或合理化：先"if it is real"才接纳；对真实的不公，书的路线是把它转为问题-目标（ch07）而非纯观想。
- "It's not a battle, but a dance"——不要把这个流程当新的战斗工具。
