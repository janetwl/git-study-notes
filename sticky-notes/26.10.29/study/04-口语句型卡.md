# 口语句型卡(辩论对话功能句)

用法:每天抽 4 组,每组用 →后的提示**替换造一个新句**,大声说出来。这套句型可直接迁移到任何话题的讨论。

## 表达观点

| 句型 | 原文 | 替换练习 |
|---|---|---|
| Have you seen how ___ ? It's incredible! | Have you seen how digital assets are revolutionizing finance? | 换成 AI / 新能源车开头 |
| I was reading about how ___ could change ___. | …digital assets could change our entire financial landscape | 读到的任何新闻 |
| I believe so! There's a growing interest, especially among ___. | …especially among younger audiences | 换人群:elderly / rural users |

## 温和反对(Nova 的核心技能)

| 句型 | 原文 | 替换练习 |
|---|---|---|
| I have, but I'm a bit skeptical about ___. | …their long-term stability | 对任何提案表疑虑 |
| That's true, but it feels like ___ . | …the wild west out there | it feels like a black box |
| A part of me thinks it's just ___ waiting to ___. | …a bubble waiting to burst | a trend waiting to fade |
| I still worry about ___ . | …people losing their hard-earned cash | students wasting time on short videos |

## 部分同意 + 转折(最高频!)

| 句型 | 原文 | 替换练习 |
|---|---|---|
| I totally get that. But don't you think ___ ? | …that's part of any emerging market? | 任何"先让步再反问" |
| Fair point! But think of how ___ and yet ___. | …banks have been hacked…yet they remain in business | 用类比反驳 |
| It could be seen that way, but ___ is exciting! | the potential for decentralization… | 任何"风险有,但收益更大" |
| That's a valid perspective. I can see how ___. | …digital assets could empower people | 承认对方合理 |
| Couldn't agree more. But ___ . | …I still worry about… | 同意+补充担忧 |

## 强调与比喻

| 句型 | 原文 | 替换练习 |
|---|---|---|
| ___ is definitely a big concern. | Security is definitely a big concern. | Privacy / Cost |
| The whole thing could come crashing down in no time. | 关于 hack | 关于信誉崩塌 |
| The hype can overshadow the reality. | C2 | 任何被炒作的产品 |
| So many people are jumping in without a clue. | C2 | 换领域:考研/创业 |

## 收尾与存疑

| 句型 | 原文 | 替换练习 |
|---|---|---|
| Honestly, if we can figure out ___, I might feel a lot more optimistic. | …(environmental concerns) | 任何"满足条件才看好" |
| I guess that's a silver lining! | C1/C2 | 坏事里找好消息 |
| Only time will tell how this all unfolds. | C2 | 万能存疑结尾 |
| I suppose we just have to ride the wave and see how it goes. | C2 | 顺势观望 |

## 迷你对话演练(脱稿)

用上面的句型,就"**AI 会取代银行柜员吗**"演 10 轮:你扮演 Alloy(乐观),再用手机录音换演 Nova(怀疑)。听回放,数一数你每组句型用了几次——目标:至少用上 8 个不同句型。
